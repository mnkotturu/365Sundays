Our Services

Providing comprehensive wealth management and advisory services to clients across the United States.

At 365Sundays Wealth LLC, we provide integrated, fiduciary guidance designed to address every dimension of your financial life. Whether you engage us for ongoing wealth management or targeted strategic planning, our solutions are tailored entirely to your goals.

PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT

Disciplined Asset Allocation

+ We design and implement custom portfolios grounded in Modern Portfolio Theory, aligning your investments precisely with your risk tolerance and long-term time horizon.

Continuous Monitoring & Rebalancing

+ Portfolios are systematically overseen and rebalanced to maintain your target risk profile across changing market cycles.

RETIREMENT PLANNING

Sustainable Withdrawal Strategies

+ We help portfolio savers and spenders determine a sustainable level of spending and stay close to it when not earning a salary.

Account Sequencing

+ We provide guidance on the order of saving and spending based on the various account types available to optimize your cash flow.

TAX & ESTATE PLANNING

Tax-Efficient Positioning

+ Being familiar with all your assets allows us to identify opportunities that may reduce income and estate taxes.

Estate Plan Alignment

+ Comprehensive reviews of your legacy framework, coordinating seamlessly with your estate attorneys to ensure proper trust and will implementation.

Concentrated Stock & Equity Compensation

+ Sophisticated risk mitigation, equity compensation planning (ISOs, NSOs, RSUs, ESPPs), and single-stock management.

EDUCATION PLANNING

Targeted Funding

+ We help parents and grandparents develop and implement a plan for college expenses.

Tax-Advantaged Vehicles

+ Strategic selection of accounts like 529 plans to support children's and grandchildren's post-secondary goals.

CHARITABLE PLANNING

Philanthropic Strategies

+ Many of our clients have been successful and enjoy making the world a better place through giving.

Tax Optimization

+ We help you maximize gifts while minimizing taxes and administrative burdens.

BUSINESS OWNERS

Practice & Corporate Advisory

+ Business owners are faced with many unique challenges and opportunities. We help these leaders see and execute unique opportunities, including start-up, growth, and the eventual sale of their business.

Retirement Plan Consulting & Management

+ Establishing, reviewing, and providing fiduciary oversight for corporate retirement structures such as 401(k) and profit-sharing plans for professional practices and small businesses.

RISK MANAGEMENT

Insurance Stack Analysis

+ We seek to understand your insurance stack to ensure adequate life insurance coverage as well as inspect for gaps in property, casualty, and liability insurance coverage.

SPECIALTY SERVICES

Executive & Career Succession Support

+ For clients who have reached the pinnacle of their professional journey and accumulated an intricate web of personal responsibilities, we step in to streamline your day-to-day duties and lay the groundwork for smooth, organized succession.

Estate & Partnership Obligations

+ We assist family members in navigating both the immediate and long-range duties required for Family Limited Partnerships and complex estate structures.
