Transparent, Fiduciary Pricing

At 365Sundays Wealth LLC, we believe in absolute transparency. As a fee-only, fiduciary firm, we do not earn commissions or hidden kickbacks—our only compensation comes directly from you.

1. Wealth Management Services (Assets Under Management)

For ongoing, comprehensive portfolio management and continuous investment oversight, our fees are billed quarterly in arrears as a percentage of assets under management (AUM).

Asset Tier Annualized Fee
$0 – $500,000 1.25%
$500,001 – $1,000,000 1.10%
$1,000,001 – $2,000,000 1.00%
$2,000,001 – $3,000,000 0.90%
$3,000,001 – $4,000,000 0.85%
$4,000,001 – $5,000,000 0.80%
$5,000,000 and Above 0.75%

Note: Fees are calculated using a blended tiered schedule, and household accounts can be aggregated to optimize your tier.

3. Retirement Plan Consulting & Management (For Businesses)

For employer plan sponsors, corporations, and professional practices (such as medical or dental offices) establishing or managing company-sponsored retirement plans like 401(k)s or profit-sharing structures, our annualized fees are based on a straight-tier asset schedule billed quarterly in arrears:

Plan Asset Tier Annualized Fee
$0 – $1,000,000 1.00%
$1,000,001 – $5,000,000 0.80%
$5,000,000 and Above 0.70%

Note: This corporate fee structure does not include third-party administrator, recordkeeper, or custodian fees.
